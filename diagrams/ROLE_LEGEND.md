# Role Legend

- `System Admin`: platform governance, RBAC policy publishing, global settings
- `Admin`: tenant/company/user management, content governance, publishing
- `Manager`: review and publish authority, analytics and feedback management
- `Editor`: document/versions authoring, peer review, collaboration editing
- `Viewer`: internal read-only user with comments and collaboration read access
- `Customer`: company-scoped portal user, document consumption and feedback
- `Public User`: anonymous access to public published content
